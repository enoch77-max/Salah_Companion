### Core Prerequisites of Salah (Shuroot as-Salah)

Before the prayer even begins, certain entry conditions must be fulfilled for the prayer to be valid under the Islamic Shariah 1\.

* **Islam:** The worshipper must be a Muslim 1\.  
* **Aql (Sanity):** The person must be of sound mind and conscious 1\.  
* **Tamyeez (Age of Understanding):** Directed at those who have reached the age of discernment (mandated starting at age seven, and becoming strictly obligatory by age ten) 1\.  
* **Taharah (Ritual Purity):** Freedom from major impurities (requiring *ghusl*, a full ritual bath) and minor impurities (requiring *wudu*, ablution) 2\.  
* **Izalat an-Najasa:** Removal of physical impurities from the body, clothing, and the spot of prayer 2, 3\.  
* **Covering the Awrah:** For males, this is from the navel to the knees 2, 4\. For females, the entire body must be covered except for the face and hands 2\.  
* **Dukhul al-Waqt:** The specific prayer's prescribed timeframe must have commenced 2, 5\.  
* **Istiqbal al-Qiblah:** Directly facing the Qiblah (direction of the Kaaba in Makkah) 2, 6, 7\.  
* **Niyyah (Intention):** Resolving in the heart to perform the specific prayer 2, 8, 9\. **The intention must strictly remain in the heart; uttering it audibly is a religious innovation (bid'ah) without authentic basis in the Sunnah 8, 10, 11\.**

### 1\. What is Necessary: The 14 Pillars of Salah (Arkan as-Salah)

Pillars are the essential, structural foundations of prayer 12-14. **If a pillar is omitted—whether deliberately or accidentally out of forgetfulness—the entire unit (Rakah) or prayer becomes invalid.** It cannot be compensated for with a Prostration of Forgetfulness (*Sujood as-Sahw*); the worshipper must perform the missing actions or repeat the entire prayer 12, 14-16.

1. **Standing (Qiyam):** Standing upright during obligatory prayers for those who are physically able 16-18. If a person is genuinely unable, they may pray sitting or lying on their side 2, 16-18.  
2. **The Opening Takbir (Takbirat al-Ihram):** Commencing the prayer by audibly saying **"Allahu Akbar"** 2, 7, 16, 17\.  
3. **Reciting Surah Al-Fatiha:** Reciting the opening chapter of the Quran in every single Rakah of the prayer 16-19. The Prophet ﷺ said: *"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book."* 19, 20  
4. **Bowing (Ruku'):** Bending forward from the waist, placing the hands securely on the knees, keeping the back flat and the head level with the spine 16, 17, 20, 21\.  
5. **Rising from Bowing:** Returning to the upright standing position 2, 16, 17\.  
6. **Standing Upright (Qawmah / I'tidal):** Pausing and standing completely straight after bowing until all joints settle back into their natural resting positions 2, 17, 22, 23\.  
7. **Prostration (Sujud):** Placing the body on the floor twice in every unit of prayer 16, 24\. **Sujud must be performed firmly on seven limbs (bones): the forehead & nose (which count as one), the two palms, the two knees, and the toes of both feet** 24-26.  
8. **Rising from Prostration:** Elevating the upper body from the first sujood 16, 24, 27\.  
9. **Sitting Between the Two Prostrations:** Bending the left foot to sit on it while keeping the right foot propped up, pausing in a brief, calm sitting posture 16, 24, 27, 28\.  
10. **Tranquility (At-Tuma'ninah) in All Actions:** Achieving a distinct moment of stillness, calm, and settlement in every physical transition (Ruku', standing upright, Sujood, and sitting) 16, 23, 28-30. **Rushing through movements renders the prayer legally void.** This is established in the famous Hadith of the "Man Who Prayed Badly" (*Musi' al-Salat*), whom the Prophet ﷺ ordered to repeat his prayer three times because he rushed 6, 22, 23, 31\.  
11. **The Final Tashahhud:** Reciting the prescribed verbal supplications during the last sitting of the prayer 16, 28, 29\.  
12. **Sitting for the Final Tashahhud:** The physical act of sitting for the final verbal recitations 16, 28, 29\.  
13. **Sending Blessings on the Prophet ﷺ (Salawat / Durood-e-Ibrahim):** Reciting the blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ and his family in the final sitting 16, 28, 32, 33\.  
14. **The Concluding Salam (Taslim):** Saying **"As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah"** to exit the prayer 16, 27, 29, 34, 35\.  
15. **Maintaining the Proper Order (At-Tarteeb):** Sequential, chronologically correct execution of all the pillars as listed 16, 29, 35, 36\.

### 2\. What is Must-Do: The Obligations of Salah (Waajibaat as-Salah)

Obligatory acts are mandatory requirements that are legally subordinate to the Pillars 14, 36, 37\.

* **If omitted deliberately:** The prayer is invalidated and must be repeated 15, 27, 37\.  
* **If omitted out of forgetfulness:** The prayer remains valid, and the worshipper does not repeat the action 15, 27, 37\. Instead, the omission is corrected by performing the Prostration of Forgetfulness (*Sujood as-Sahw*) at the end of the prayer 15, 27, 36-38.  
1. **All Transitional Takbirs:** Saying **"Allahu Akbar"** when moving between positions (except for the opening Takbir, which is a Pillar) 27, 36, 37\.  
2. **The Tasmee':** Saying **"Sami'allahu liman hamidah"** (Allah hears the one who praises Him) when rising from Ruku' 21, 27, 36, 37\. *(This is mandatory for the Imam and the solo worshipper)* 27, 39\.  
3. **The Tahmeed:** Saying **"Rabbanaa wa lakal-hamd"** (Our Lord, to You belongs all praise) when standing upright after bowing 21, 27, 36, 37\.  
4. **Glorification in Bowing:** Reciting **"Subhaana Rabbiyal-Adheem"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Supreme) at least once during Ruku' 21, 27, 36, 37\.  
5. **Glorification in Prostration:** Reciting **"Subhaana Rabbiyal-A'laa"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High) at least once during Sujood 25, 27, 36, 37\.  
6. **Supplication Between Prostrations:** Saying **"Rabbigh-fir lee"** (My Lord, forgive me) at least once while sitting between the two prostrations 25, 27, 36, 37\.  
7. **The First Tashahhud:** Reciting the specific *Attahiyyaat* supplication in the middle sitting (e.g., in a 3 or 4 Rakah prayer) 27, 32, 36, 37\.  
8. **Sitting for the First Tashahhud:** The physical act of sitting to recite the first Tashahhud 27, 36, 37\.

### 3\. What is Sunnah: Recommended Acts (Sunan as-Salah)

Sunnah acts are optional, recommended practices modeled after the Prophet's ﷺ personal prayer habits 24, 40\. **Omiting these acts—whether intentionally or accidentally—has no effect on the validity of the prayer, and does not require Sujood as-Sahw 40, 41\.**

#### A. Verbal Sunnah (Sunan Qawliyyah)

* **The Opening Supplication (Dua al-Istiftaah / Thana):** Recited silently after the opening Takbir and before Al-Fatiha 7, 40, 41\. An authentic narration is: *"Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka, wa la ilaha ghairuk"* 7, 42-44.  
* **Ta'awwudh and Basmalah:** Silently saying *"A'udhu billahi..."* (Seeking refuge from Satan) and *"Bismillah..."* before starting Al-Fatiha 40, 41, 45, 46\.  
* **Saying Ameen:** Saying **"Ameen"** silently or aloud after finishing Surah Al-Fatiha 40, 41, 45, 47\.  
* **Reciting an Additional Surah:** Reciting any portion of the Quran after Al-Fatiha during the first two Rakahat 40, 41, 45\.  
* **Additional Tasbeeh:** Reciting the glorifications in Ruku' and Sujood more than the mandatory single time (e.g., three times or more in odd numbers) 40, 41\.  
* **Final Supplications:** Asking Allah for protection and well-being after the final Tashahhud and Salawat, such as seeking refuge from the four trials (punishment of the grave, punishment of Hellfire, trials of life & death, and the trials of the Dajjal) 41, 48-50.

#### B. Action Sunnah (Sunan Fi'liyyah)

* **Raising the Hands (Raf' al-Yadayn):** Raising the hands up to the level of the shoulders or the earlobes 7, 40, 51\. This is done at **four specific transition points**:  
* With the opening Takbir 40, 51, 52\.  
* Before descending into Ruku' 40, 51, 52\.  
* Upon rising from Ruku' 40, 51, 52\.  
* When standing up for the third Rakah after the first Tashahhud 40, 51, 52\.  
* **Placing Hands on the Chest:** Folding the arms during standing (Qiyam), placing the right hand over the left hand, wrist, and forearm, positioned directly upon the chest 40, 51, 53-55.  
* **Fixing the Gaze:** Looking down specifically at the place of prostration during standing to maintain focus 40, 51, 56, 57\.  
* **Spreading the Fingers:** Keeping the fingers spread wide while holding the knees during Ruku' 21, 51, 58\.  
* **Sitting Postures (Iftirash & Tawarruk):**  
* **Iftirash:** Sitting on the left foot while keeping the right foot upright (used in the sitting between the two prostrations and in the first Tashahhud) 40, 51, 59, 60\.  
* **Tawarruk:** Sitting with the left thigh flat on the ground, pushing the left foot out from under the right leg, and sitting directly on the left buttock (used in the final Tashahhud sitting of a 3 or 4 Rakah prayer) 40, 51, 60-62.  
* **The Tashahhud Finger Gesture:** Making a ring with the thumb and middle finger and pointing the index finger of the right hand toward the Qiblah, raising or moving it slightly during the recitation of the Tashahhud 63-65.

### 4\. What You Can Avoid: Common Errors & Prohibited Actions

To ensure the spiritual and physical integrity of the prayer, worshippers must avoid these common errors, custom innovations, and detested actions 66, 67:

* **Audible Intention:** Uttering verbal formulas of intention (e.g., *"I intend to pray..."*) is a religious innovation (*bid'ah*). The intention is strictly an act of the heart 8, 10\.  
* **Fidgeting and Moving Excessively (Amal Kathir):** Continuous non-prayer movements (adjusting clothing, fixing hats, checking watches, playing with hair) without genuine necessity can invalidate the prayer 67-70.  
* **Incomplete Sujood:** Accidental invalidation by lifting feet, toes, or hands off the ground during prostration, or failing to let the nose touch the floor firmly alongside the forehead 26, 71\.  
* **Unnecessary Eye Closing:** The Prophet ﷺ kept his eyes open in prayer 72, 73\. Closing the eyes is only recommended if there is a severe distraction directly in front of the worshipper 73\.  
* **Spreading Arms Like a Dog:** Spreading the forearms flat on the ground during Sujood. Worshippers must lift their elbows off the floor and keep them away from their sides 25, 74, 75\.  
* **Looking Around/Upwards:** Looking up at the sky or looking side-to-side during prayer is forbidden. The Prophet ﷺ strongly warned that doing so risks having one's eyesight taken away 51, 76, 77\.  
* **Swaying and Stepping (Dancing):** Stepping repeatedly to match others' feet or moving side-to-side in a way that disrupts stillness and *khushu'* (humility) 78, 79\.  
* **Failing to Use a Sutrah:** Praying without placing a barrier (like a wall, pillar, or object) closely in front of you when praying alone, which leaves your prayer vulnerable to being interrupted 80-82.  
* **Wiping the Face Post-Taslim:** Immediately wiping the forehead with the hand right after completing the salam has no basis in the Sunnah 83\.  
* **Verbal/Physical Gestures in Salam:** Pointing the hands to the right and left while turning the head for Taslim 84-86. The hands must remain flat on the thighs 87, 88\.
