# The Ultimate step-by-step Guide: Praying as the Prophet ﷺ Prayed

### Step 0: Preparing Your Heart and Body (Prerequisites)

Before you even step onto your prayer mat, you must prepare yourself externally and internally to stand before the Lord of the worlds 2, 3\.

1. **Taharah (Ritual Purity):** Ensure you are free from physical impurities 4, 5\. Cleanse your body, clothes, and the spot of prayer 4-6. As the Prophet ﷺ declared in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 223**: *"Purification is half of faith."* 7, 8 To make your prayer valid, you must perform a perfect Wudu (ablution) 5, 9\. **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6954** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 225** record: *"Allah does not accept the prayer of any of you who is in a state of impurity until he performs Wudu."* 10  
2. **Covering the Awrah:** Cover your private areas appropriately 4-6. For men, this is from the navel to the knees; for women, the entire body must be covered except for the face and hands 6\.  
3. **Facing the Qiblah:** Stand upright facing the direction of the Ka'bah in Makkah 6, 11, 12\.  
4. **Intention (Niyyah):** Resolve in your heart which prayer you are performing 6, 11\. **Do not speak your intention aloud.** Uttering the intention verbally is a religious innovation (bid'ah) that has no authentic basis in the Sunnah 13, 14\. As recorded in **Zad al-Ma'ad**, the Prophet ﷺ never uttered his intention out loud before commencing 15, 16\. Your heart is fully sufficient 14\.

### Step 1: Commencing with Majesty (Takbirat al-Ihram & Qiyam)

Now, you are ready to enter your sacred audience with Allah 8, 17\.

1. **Stand upright (Qiyam):** Standing is an essential pillar of obligatory prayers if you are physically able 6, 18\. Allah commands in **Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 238**: *"And stand before Allah with devotion."* 18, 19 If you are unable to stand due to illness or injury, you are fully permitted to pray sitting or lying on your side 6, 18, 20\.  
2. **Fix your gaze:** Cast your eyes down at the place where your forehead will rest in Sujud 21-23. Looking up or around is strictly forbidden 21, 24, 25\. In **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 750**, the Prophet ﷺ sternly warned: *"They should stop (looking towards the sky during the Salat); otherwise their eyesight would be snatched away."* 22, 23, 26  
3. **Raise your hands:** Raise your hands level with your shoulders or your earlobes, with your palms facing the Qiblah 6, 11, 27\. As recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 390a**, this was the consistent practice of the Prophet ﷺ 28\.  
4. **The Opening Takbir:** Say **"Allahu Akbar"** (Allah is the Greatest) audibly to commence your prayer 6, 27, 29\. This is a pillar of Salah 30, 31\. As the Prophet ﷺ taught in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 61** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3**: *"The key to prayer is purification; its beginning is the Takbeer and its end is the Tasleem."* 5, 32, 33  
5. **Fold your hands:** Place your right hand over your left hand, wrist, and forearm directly upon your chest 27, 34-36. As recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 740**, Sahl bin Sa'd reported: *"The people were ordered to place the right hand on the left forearm in the prayer."* 36-38 In **Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 890**, Wa'il bin Hujr narrated: *"He placed his right hand over his left hand, wrist, and lower forearm."* 36, 39 To follow the Sunnah perfectly, rest them on your chest, as recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 759** 23, 40, 41\.

### Step 2: The Divine Dialogue (The Recitations)

1. **Opening Supplication (Dua al-Istiftah):** Recite the opening supplication silently 11, 27, 29\. An authentic option recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 775** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 243** is: *"Subhanakallahumma wa bihamdika wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghairuk"* 10, 42\.  
2. **Seek refuge:** Silently seek refuge from Shaitan by saying: **"A'udhu billahi minash-shaitanir-rajim"** 43\. Allah commands in **Surah An-Nahl, Verse 98**: *"When you recite the Quran, seek refuge with Allah from the accursed Shaitan."* 43, 44  
3. **Say the Basmalah:** Silently recite **"Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim"** 43, 45\. In **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 743** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 399**, Anas reported that the Prophet ﷺ, Abu Bakr, and Umar began their public audible recitation directly with the opening of Surah Al-Fatiha, meaning the Bismillah is recited silently 45-47.  
4. **Recite Surah Al-Fatiha:** Recite the opening chapter of the Quran in every single Rakah of your prayer 18, 30, 48\. This is a non-negotiable pillar 30, 48\. The Prophet ﷺ said in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 756** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 394**: *"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book (Surah Al-Fatiha)."* 48-50 Pause at the end of each verse, reciting with calm reflection 51-53.  
5. **Say Ameen:** After finishing Surah Al-Fatiha, say **"Ameen"** 54, 55\. In **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 780**, the Prophet ﷺ said: *"When the Imam says: 'Ghairil-maghdubi alayhim walad-daalleen', then say 'Ameen', for if one's utterance of 'Ameen' coincides with that of the angels, then his past sins will be forgiven."* 55, 56 Extend and prolong your voice when saying Ameen 57, 58\.  
6. **Recite an extra Surah:** In the first two Rakahs of your prayer, recite any extra Surah or passage from the Quran after Al-Fatiha 54, 59, 60\. Al-Fatiha is the only obligatory part; reciting more is a rewarded Sunnah 54, 59, 61\.

### Step 3: Bowing in Humility (Ruku')

1. **The transition Takbir:** Say **"Allahu Akbar"** to transition to bowing 62-64.  
2. **Raise your hands:** Raise your hands level with your shoulders/earlobes for Raf' al-Yadayn as you make this Takbir 65-68. As recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735**, Salim narrated that his father saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ raise his hands when making the Takbir for bowing 28\.  
3. **Perform Ruku':** Bend forward from your waist 20, 69, 70\. Keep your back flat and straight, and your head level with your spine (neither hanging down nor lifted up) 20, 50, 70, 71\. Your hands should grasp your knees with your fingers spread wide 20, 70, 72\.  
4. **Tranquility is key:** As recorded in **Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 884** (the Hadith of the Musi' al-Salat/Man Who Prayed Badly), you must achieve absolute stillness (Tuma'ninah) in this position; do not rush 73-75.  
5. **Glorification:** While bowing, say **"Subhana Rabbiyal-Adheem"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Supreme) 62, 76, 77\. Fulfill the obligation by saying it at least once 62, 78, 79\. Saying it three times is the recommended Sunnah, based on **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 855** 57, 62, 78, 80\.

### Step 4: Standing Straight in Praise (I'tidal)

1. **Rise up:** Straighten your back toward an upright standing position 20, 76, 81\.  
2. **Raise your hands & praise:** Raise your hands for Raf' al-Yadayn and say: **"Sami'allahu liman hamidah"** (Allah hears the one who praises Him) 62, 65, 68, 82\. This is mandatory for the Imam and the solo worshipper 62, 83\. **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735** records the Prophet ﷺ saying this when raising his head from bowing 28, 84\.  
3. **Declare your gratitude:** Once standing completely straight, say: **"Rabbanaa wa lakal-hamd"** (Our Lord, and to You belongs all praise) 62, 76, 81\. Achieve complete stillness (Tuma'ninah) until all of your backbones and vertebrae return to their natural resting positions 81, 85, 86\. Rushing to prostrate before standing completely straight invalidates the prayer 87-90. The Prophet ﷺ taught in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 757**: *"Then raise your head and stand up straight..."* 91, 92

### Step 5: The Ultimate Surrender (Sujud)

1. **The transition:** Say **"Allahu Akbar"** and descend into prostration 62, 69\. You do not raise your hands for Raf' al-Yadayn during this transition 28, 69\.  
2. **Descending:** You may place your knees down on the floor first, followed by your hands, forehead, and nose 81, 82\. This is reported by Wa'il bin Hujr in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 838** 82, 93, 94\. Alternatively, you may place your hands down first 82, 93, 95\. Scholars agree both ways are perfectly valid 93\.  
3. **The 7 bones of Sujud:** You must prostrate firmly on **seven specific limbs (bones)**: your forehead and nose (which count as one), your two palms, your two knees, and the toes of both feet 96-98. This is strictly commanded by the Prophet ﷺ in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 812** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1098**: *"I have been commanded to prostrate on seven bones: the forehead (and he pointed to his nose), the two hands, the two knees, and the toes of both feet..."* 96, 97, 99, 100  
4. **Keep elbows up:** Keep your forearms elevated off the floor and your elbows away from your sides 69, 101\. **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 822** states: *"Straighten yourselves when doing Sujud, never spread your arms on the ground as dogs do."* 101  
5. **Glorification:** While in Sujud, say: **"Subhana Rabbiyal-A'laa"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High) 62, 76, 77\. Fulfill the obligation by saying it at least once 62\. Reciting it three times is the Sunnah 6, 69, 77, 102\. Achieve absolute tranquility (Tuma'ninah) 87, 103, 104\.

### Step 6: Sitting Between Prostrations (Jalsah)

1. **Sit up:** Say **"Allahu Akbar"** and sit up straight 62, 63\.  
2. **The Iftirash posture:** Sit in the **Iftirash** posture: bend your left foot to sit on it while keeping your right foot upright with the toes pointing toward the Qiblah 59, 87, 105, 106\. Place your hands flat on your thighs and knees 105-108.  
3. **Achieve stillness:** Calmly pause until every bone returns to its resting place 85, 87, 90\.  
4. **Supplication:** Say: **"Rabbigh-fir lee"** (My Lord, forgive me) 62, 76, 109\. Say it at least once to fulfill the obligation 62\. Saying it three times (or more) is recommended 69, 109\. This is recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 874** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 284** 62\.  
5. **Second prostration:** Perform your second Sujud exactly like the first, saying the same glorifications 69, 87, 102, 110\.

### Step 7: Rising for the Next Rakah

1. **The sitting of rest:** Upon rising from the second Sujud, sit briefly and calmly for a moment of rest before standing 111-113. This is called **Jalsah al-Istiraha** (the sitting of rest), as modeled by the Prophet ﷺ in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 828** 6, 106, 112-114.  
2. **Rise up:** Stand up for the next Rakah 115\. You can support yourself by placing your clenched fists (like kneading dough) or flat palms on the ground 111\.  
3. **The next Rakah:** Perform the second Rakah exactly like the first 86, 87, 116, 117\. Note: You do not repeat Dua al-Istiftah or the opening seek of refuge in subsequent Rakahs 117\.

### Step 8: The Sittings and Tashahhud

1. **The first sitting:** If your prayer is 3 or 4 Rakahs, sit after the second Rakah to recite the **First Tashahhud** 62, 109\. Sit in the **Iftirash** posture 59\.  
2. **The recitation:** Recite the *Attahiyyaat* supplication 62, 109\. An authentic version taught by Ibn Mas'ud in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 835** is: *"At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat..."* 84, 118, 119\.  
3. **Finger gesture:** Place your right hand on your right thigh and your left hand on your left thigh 105-107. Form a ring with your thumb and middle finger, and point your right index finger towards the Qiblah 59, 107, 119\. Point your index finger and move or shake it slightly in its position to supplicate, as recorded in **Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 1219** 59, 120-123.  
4. **The final sitting:** In the **Final Tashahhud** of a 3 or 4 Rakah prayer, sit in the **Tawarruk** posture: sit directly on your left buttock with your left thigh flat on the ground, pushing your left foot out under your right leg, and keeping your right foot propped up 59, 82, 106\. This is recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 828** 6, 106, 119, 124\.  
5. **Blessings on the Prophet ﷺ:** Recite the blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ (**Salawat/Durood-e-Ibrahim**), recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3369** 125\.  
6. **Seek protection:** After the blessings, make personal supplication before saying the Salam 54, 59, 126\. The Prophet ﷺ recommended seeking refuge from the four trials in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 835** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1324**: *"O Allah, I seek refuge with You from the punishment of the grave, the punishment of Hellfire, the trials of life and death, and the trials of the Dajjal."* 54, 127-129

### Step 9: Concluding with Peace (Taslim)

1. **The Taslim:** Turn your head fully to the right until the whiteness of your cheek is visible to those behind you, saying: **"As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah"** 76, 128, 130\. Then turn your head to the left and say the same 128, 130, 131\.  
2. **The end of prayer:** This concludes your Salah 5, 32\. **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 61** states: *"...and its end is the Tasleem."* 5, 32, 33  
3. **Do not wave your hands:** Do not make physical waving gestures with your hands to the sides during the Salam 132, 133\. This is strictly prohibited in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 431**: *"What is wrong with them that they make salutation with their hands as if they were the tails of headstrong horses? Place your hand on your thigh..."* 132-134

### Step 10: Common Pitfalls to Avoid

To make sure your prayers are complete, avoid these common mistakes:

* **Verbally reciting your intention:** Spoken intentions (e.g., "I intend to pray four Rakahs...") have no basis in the authentic Sunnah; intention is strictly an act of the heart 14, 15\.  
* **Rushing through the actions:** Rushing through bowing, standing, prostrating, and sitting invalidates your Salah 87-90. Stillness and tranquility (Tuma'ninah) in every position are compulsory pillars 87, 90, 104\.  
* **Letting your eyes wander:** Keep your gaze fixed down on your prostration spot 21-23. Looking up at the sky or looking side-to-side is strictly forbidden 21, 24, 25\.  
* **Flat Sujud:** Spreading your forearms flat on the ground during prostration like a dog is prohibited 69, 101\. Keep your elbows raised and away from your body 69, 101\.  
* **Lifting your feet in Sujud:** Ensure all seven limbs (including both feet and your nose/forehead) stay firmly in contact with the ground during Sujud 96-98, 135\.
