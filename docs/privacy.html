Privacy Policy for Salah Companion

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

1. Strict Zero-Tracking Architecture

Salah Companion is designed from the ground up to respect your spiritual focus and personal privacy. We do not collect, transmit, store, share, or sell any personal data or behavioral analytics. The application contains zero commercial SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking scripts.

2. Location Permission & Usage

GPS and network location permissions are requested exclusively to calculate astronomical prayer times and determine Qibla direction for your current coordinates.

Local-Only Processing: Your geographical coordinates are processed 100% locally on your device. Coordinates are never transmitted off-device to any cloud server, third-party API, or backend service.

3. Local Storage & Data Retention

All user preferences, calculation settings, prayer logs, and Tasbih counts are stored strictly within an encrypted SQLite database on your local device storage. You maintain 100% ownership and control over your data. Uninstalling the application completely deletes all local data.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

Salah Companion operates entirely offline. It does not integrate third-party authentication services, cloud backup services, or ad networks.

5. Open Source Code Auditability

Salah Companion is open-source under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. The full codebase is available for public audit on GitHub: https://github.com/enoch77-max/Salah_Companion

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository or contact the maintainer at GitHub Repository Issues.
