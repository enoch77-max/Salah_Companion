Privacy Policy

Salah Companion — Your privacy is sacred, not an afterthought.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 · Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Salah Companion ("we", "our", or "the app") is an authentic, offline-first Islamic prayer times, Qibla compass, Tasbih counter, and Hadith companion application developed by enoch77-max.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your information. This policy applies to all versions of Salah Companion distributed through Google Play and other platforms.

2. Data We Collect

None.

Salah Companion operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking mechanisms of any kind.

✅ Zero advertisements · Zero third-party analytics · Zero user accounts required · Zero behavioral tracking

3. Local Data Storage

All data generated by the app — including prayer calculation preferences, logged prayers, Tasbih counts, madhab settings, and user preferences — is stored strictly on your local device in an encrypted SQLite database.

You remain in complete control of your data at all times. No data is ever synced, backed up, or transmitted to any cloud service or remote server.

4. Permissions We Request

The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core functionality. All permissions are optional — the app functions without them, with reduced features.

📍 Location Optional
Used exclusively to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your geographic position. Location data is processed entirely on-device using the local adhan_dart library and is never transmitted anywhere.
🔔 Notifications Optional
Used to deliver prayer time reminders (Adhan alerts). All notification scheduling is handled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.
⏰ Exact Alarm Android 12+
Required on Android 12 and above to schedule precise, time-critical prayer notifications. No data is collected or sent externally through this permission.

5. Third-Party Services

Salah Companion does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, or data-collection services.

  • Google Fonts (Inter): Typography fonts are bundled with the app at build time and do not make network requests at runtime.
  • System Share Sheet: The share functionality uses the native operating system share dialog. We do not intercept or log shared content.
  • Platform Geocoding: Reverse geocoding (converting coordinates to place names) uses the built-in platform APIs (Google Play Services on Android, CoreLocation on iOS) and does not involve any custom third-party service.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over data retention and deletion:

  • Clear App Data: Go to your device Settings → Apps → Salah Companion → Storage → Clear Data to remove all stored preferences and prayer data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app completely and permanently deletes all locally stored data.
  • No remote data exists: Since we never collect or transmit data, there is no server-side data to request deletion of.

7. Your Rights

Because Salah Companion collects zero personal data and stores everything locally on your device, you inherently retain full rights over your information:

  • Right to Access: All your data is stored on your device and accessible through the app at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can modify your prayer settings, preferences, and logged data directly within the app.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all data by clearing app data or uninstalling the application.
  • Right to Data Portability: Your data lives on your device and is not locked behind any account or cloud service.

8. Children's Privacy

Salah Companion does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the app collects zero personal data and operates entirely offline, it is safe for users of all ages. No age verification is required because no personal information is gathered.

9. Security

Your data is protected by the security measures built into your device's operating system. The app stores data in an encrypted SQLite database. Since no data is transmitted over the internet, there is no risk of data interception during transmission.
