## 1. Introduction
Salah Companion ("we", "our", or "the app") is an authentic, offline-first Islamic prayer times, Qibla compass, Tasbih counter, and Hadith companion application developed by **enoch77-max**.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your information. This policy applies to all versions of Salah Companion distributed through Google Play and other platforms.

---

## 2. Zero-Tracking Commitment & Data Collection
**We collect no personal data.**

Salah Companion operates entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking mechanisms of any kind.

- **Zero Advertisements**: No ad SDKs, banner ads, interstitial ads, or promotional tracking.
- **Zero Third-Party Analytics**: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
- **Zero User Accounts**: No sign-up, login, email registration, or user profiling.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & Security
All data generated by the app—including prayer calculation preferences, logged prayers, Tasbih counts, madhab settings, and user preferences—is stored **strictly on your local device** in an encrypted SQLite database (`drift`).

Your data remains in your complete control at all times. No data is ever synced, backed up, or transmitted to any cloud service or remote server.

---

## 4. Permissions We Request
The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core Islamic companion functionality. All permissions are optional:

1. **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) — Optional**:
   - Used exclusively to calculate astronomical prayer times and determine Qibla direction for your geographic position.
   - Processed 100% locally on-device via the `adhan_dart` library.
   - **Never uploaded** to any remote server or third party.
2. **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) — Optional**:
   - Used to deliver prayer time reminders (Adhan alerts).
   - All notification scheduling is handled locally on your device via `flutter_local_notifications`.
3. **Exact Alarm (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`) — Android 12+**:
   - Required to schedule precise, time-critical prayer notifications.
   - No data is collected or sent externally.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
Salah Companion does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection SDKs:
- **Google Fonts (Inter / Amiri)**: Typography fonts are bundled within the app assets at build time and do not make runtime network requests.
- **System Share Sheet**: Uses the native operating system share dialog. We do not intercept or log shared content.
- **Platform Geocoding**: Reverse geocoding (coordinates to city name) uses built-in operating system APIs (Google Play Services on Android, CoreLocation on iOS).

---

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion Policy
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- **To delete your data**: Clear app data via your device's **Settings → Apps → Salah Companion → Storage → Clear Data**, or simply uninstall the application.
- **Uninstalling the app** completely and permanently deletes all locally stored data.
- **No server-side data exists**: Because no data is ever transmitted, there is no remote data to request deletion of.

---

## 7. Your Rights
Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards, you maintain full ownership and control:
- **Right to Access**: All data is viewable inside the app on your device.
- **Right to Rectification**: Modify any calculation settings, prayer logs, or preferences directly within the app.
- **Right to Deletion / Erasure**: Clear app data or uninstall the application at any time.
- **Right to Portability**: Your data resides on your device without account lock-in.

Salah Companion does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app is completely offline and collects zero personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.
