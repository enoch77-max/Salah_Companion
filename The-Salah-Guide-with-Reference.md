### Core Prerequisites of Salah (Shuroot as-Salah)

Before the prayer even begins, certain entry conditions must be fulfilled for the prayer to be valid under the Islamic Shariah, as established in the authentic text of **Surah An-Nisa, Verse 103** which states: *"Indeed, the prayer has been enjoined upon the believers at prescribed times."* 1-4

* **Islam:** The worshipper must be a Muslim 5, 6\.  
* **Aql (Sanity):** The person must be of sound mind and conscious 5, 6\.  
* **Tamyeez (Age of Understanding):** Directed at those who have reached the age of discernment (mandated starting at age seven, and becoming strictly obligatory by age ten) 5, 6\.  
* **Taharah (Ritual Purity):** Freedom from major impurities (requiring *ghusl*, a full ritual bath) and minor impurities (requiring *wudu*, ablution) 5, 6\. This is grounded in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 223**, which declares: *"Purification is half of faith."* 7, 8 Wudu is a non-negotiable legal condition for the validity of Salah, as affirmed in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6954** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 225**: *"Allah does not accept the prayer of any of you who is in a state of impurity until he performs Wudu."* 9  
* **Izalat an-Najasa:** Removal of physical impurities from the body, clothing, and the spot of prayer 5, 6\.  
* **Covering the Awrah:** For males, this is from the navel to the knees. For females, the entire body must be covered except for the face and hands 5, 6\.  
* **Dukhul al-Waqt:** The specific prayer's prescribed timeframe must have commenced 5, 6\.  
* **Istiqbal al-Qiblah:** Directly facing the Qiblah (direction of the Kaaba in Makkah) 5, 6\.  
* **Niyyah (Intention):** Resolving in the heart to perform the specific prayer 5, 6\. **The intention must strictly remain in the heart; uttering it audibly is a religious innovation (bid'ah) without authentic basis in the Sunnah.** 10

### 1\. What is Necessary: The 14 Pillars of Salah (Arkan as-Salah)

Pillars are the essential, structural foundations of prayer. **If a pillar is omitted—whether deliberately or accidentally out of forgetfulness—the entire unit (Rakah) or prayer becomes invalid.** 11-14 It cannot be compensated for with a Prostration of Forgetfulness (*Sujood as-Sahw*); the worshipper must perform the missing actions or repeat the entire prayer 11-15. This structure is established by the Prophet's ﷺ overarching directive in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 631**: *"Pray as you have seen me praying."* 16-19

1. **Standing (Qiyam):** Standing upright during obligatory prayers for those who are physically able 13, 20\. This is commanded in **Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 238**: *"And stand before Allah with devotion."* 21-24 If a person is genuinely unable, they may pray sitting or lying on their side 6, 20, 24\.  
2. **The Opening Takbir (Takbirat al-Ihram):** Commencing the prayer by audibly saying **"Allahu Akbar"** 13, 20\. This puts one into the sacred state of prayer, as stated in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 61** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3**: *"The key to prayer is purification; its beginning is the Takbeer and its end is the Tasleem."* 25, 26  
3. **Reciting Surah Al-Fatiha:** Reciting the opening chapter of the Quran in every single Rakah of the prayer 13, 20\. This is explicitly mandated in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 756** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 394**: *"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book (Surah Al-Fatiha)."* 27-31  
4. **Bowing (Ruku'):** Bending forward from the waist, placing the hands securely on the knees, keeping the back flat and the head level with the spine 13, 20, 32\.  
5. **Rising from Bowing:** Returning to the upright standing position 13, 20\.  
6. **Standing Upright (Qawmah / I'tidal):** Pausing and standing completely straight after bowing until all joints settle back into their natural resting positions, as described in the Prophetic description of **Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim**: *"He would raise his head from his bowing, then stand straight until all of his backbones returned to their places."* 33  
7. **Prostration (Sujud):** Placing the body on the floor twice in every unit of prayer 34\. **Sujud must be performed firmly on seven limbs (bones): the forehead & nose (which count as one), the two palms, the two knees, and the toes of both feet**. This is strictly commanded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 812** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1098**: *"I have been commanded to prostrate on seven bones: the forehead (and he pointed to his nose), the two hands, the two knees, and the toes of both feet..."* 35-37  
8. **Rising from Prostration:** Elevating the upper body from the first sujood 13\.  
9. **Sitting Between the Two Prostrations:** Bending the left foot to sit on it while keeping the right foot propped up, pausing in a brief, calm sitting posture 13, 38\.  
10. **Tranquility (At-Tuma'ninah) in All Actions:** Achieving a distinct moment of stillness, calm, and settlement in every physical transition (Ruku', standing upright, Sujood, and sitting) 13, 39\. **Rushing through movements renders the prayer legally void.** This is established in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 757 & 793** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 397** (the famous Hadith of the "Man Who Prayed Badly" / *Musi' al-Salat*), whom the Prophet ﷺ ordered to repeat his prayer three times because he rushed, stating: *"Return and pray, for you have not prayed... then bow until you are at ease in bowing... then prostrate until you are at ease in prostration..."* 40-43  
11. **The Final Tashahhud:** Reciting the prescribed verbal supplications during the last sitting of the prayer 13, 39\.  
12. **Sitting for the Final Tashahhud:** The physical act of sitting for the final verbal recitations 13, 39\.  
13. **Sending Blessings on the Prophet ﷺ (Salawat / Durood-e-Ibrahim):** Reciting the blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ and his family in the final sitting, as recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 3369**: *"O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim..."* 44-46  
14. **The Concluding Salam (Taslim):** Saying **"As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah"** to exit the prayer, as established in the hadith: *"...and its end is the Tasleem."* (recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 61** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3**). 25, 26  
15. **Maintaining the Proper Order (At-Tarteeb):** Sequential, chronologically correct execution of all the pillars as listed 13, 39, 47, 48\.

### 2\. What is Must-Do: The Obligations of Salah (Waajibaat as-Salah)

Obligatory acts are mandatory requirements that are legally subordinate to the Pillars 14, 47, 49\.

* **If omitted deliberately:** The prayer is invalidated and must be repeated 12, 14, 47, 49, 50\.  
* **If omitted out of forgetfulness:** The prayer remains valid, and the worshipper does not repeat the action 12, 14, 47, 49, 50\. Instead, the omission is corrected by performing the Prostration of Forgetfulness (*Sujood as-Sahw*) at the end of the prayer 12, 14, 47, 49, 50\.  
1. **All Transitional Takbirs:** Saying **"Allahu Akbar"** when moving between positions, such as descending to bowing or prostrating (except for the opening Takbir, which is a Pillar) 47, 49, 50\. Established in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 789**: *"The Prophet ﷺ used to recite Takbir then do Sujud and recite Takbir before standing up..."* 51  
2. **The Tasmee':** Saying **"Sami'allahu liman hamidah"** (Allah hears the one who praises Him) when rising from Ruku'. This is mandatory for the Imam and the solo worshipper, as established in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735**: *"and on raising his head from bowing he used to do the same and then say 'Sami'allahu liman hamidah'."* 52-54  
3. **The Tahmeed:** Saying **"Rabbanaa wa lakal-hamd"** (Our Lord, to You belongs all praise) when standing upright after bowing, as recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 390a**. 52, 55, 56  
4. **Glorification in Bowing:** Reciting **"Subhaana Rabbiyal-Adheem"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Supreme) at least once during Ruku', as commanded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 855**: *"When 'Glorify the name of your Lord, the Great,' was revealed, the Prophet told us, 'Do so in your bowings'."* 57  
5. **Glorification in Prostration:** Reciting **"Subhaana Rabbiyal-A'laa"** (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High) at least once during Sujood, as commanded in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 771** and **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 855**. 58, 59  
6. **Supplication Between Prostrations:** Saying **"Rabbigh-fir lee"** (My Lord, forgive me) at least once while sitting between the two prostrations, as recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 874** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 284**. 47, 49, 60  
7. **The First Tashahhud:** Reciting the specific *Attahiyyaat* supplication in the middle sitting (e.g., in a 3 or 4 Rakah prayer), as taught by the Prophet ﷺ in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 835**: *"Blessed salutations and good prayers are for Allah..."* 47, 49, 61, 62  
8. **Sitting for the First Tashahhud:** The physical act of sitting to recite the first Tashahhud 47, 49\.

### 3\. What is Sunnah: Recommended Acts (Sunan as-Salah)

Sunnah acts are optional, recommended practices modeled after the Prophet's ﷺ personal prayer habits 17, 63, 64\. **Omiting these acts—whether intentionally or accidentally—has no effect on the validity of the prayer, and does not require Sujood as-Sahw.** 17, 63, 64

#### A. Verbal Sunnah (Sunan Qawliyyah)

* **The Opening Supplication (Dua al-Istiftaah):** Recited silently after the opening Takbir and before Al-Fatiha 63, 64\. An authentic narration recorded in **Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 775** and **Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Hadith 243** is: *"Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka, wa la ilaha ghairuk"* 9, 65\. Another variation is recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 744** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 598**: *"Allahumma ba'id bayni wa bayna khatayaya..."* 66, 67  
* **Ta'awwudh and Basmalah:** Silently saying *"A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajeem"* (Seeking refuge from Satan) and *"Bismillah..."* before starting Al-Fatiha, as commanded in **Surah An-Nahl, Verse 98**: *"When you recite the Quran, seek refuge with Allah..."* 30, 63, 64, 68  
* **Saying Ameen:** Saying **"Ameen"** aloud after finishing Surah Al-Fatiha in audible prayers, as recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 782** and **Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 853**: *"When the Imam says 'Ghairil-maghdubi alayhim walad-daalleen', then say Ameen..."* 69, 70  
* **Reciting an Additional Surah:** Reciting any portion of the Quran after Al-Fatiha during the first two Rakahat 30, 63, 64\.  
* **Additional Tasbeeh:** Reciting the glorifications in Ruku' and Sujood more than the mandatory single time (e.g., three times or more in odd numbers) 50, 57, 63\.  
* **Final Supplications:** Asking Allah for protection after the final Tashahhud, as recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 835**: *"O Allah, I seek refuge with You from the punishment of the grave, and from the afflictions of Dajjal, and from the trials of life and death..."* 63, 64, 71

#### B. Action Sunnah (Sunan Fi'liyyah)

* **Raising the Hands (Raf' al-Yadayn):** Raising the hands up to the level of the shoulders or the earlobes, with fingers extended 72, 73\. This is done at **four specific transition points** (grounded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 735 & 739** and **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 390a**):  
* With the opening Takbir 52, 64, 74\.  
* Before descending into Ruku' 52, 64, 74\.  
* Upon rising from Ruku' 52, 64, 74\.  
* When standing up for the third Rakah after the first Tashahhud 55, 64, 74, 75\.  
* **Placing Hands on the Chest:** Folding the arms during standing (Qiyam), placing the right hand over the left hand, wrist, and forearm 76\. Grounded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 740**: *"The people were ordered to place the right hand on the left forearm in the prayer."* 76, 77 It is placed directly upon the chest as recorded in **Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 890** and **Sahih Ibn Khuzaymah, Hadith 479**. 76, 78, 79  
* **Fixing the Gaze:** Looking down specifically at the place of prostration during standing to maintain focus, as reported in the hadith where the Prophet ﷺ strongly warned against looking up: *"They should stop (looking towards the sky); otherwise their eyesight would be taken away."* (recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 750**). 78, 80  
* **Spreading the Fingers:** Keeping the fingers spread wide while holding the knees during Ruku', as recorded in **Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 873**. 81, 82  
* **Sitting Postures (Iftirash & Tawarruk):**  
* **Iftirash:** Sitting on the left foot while keeping the right foot upright. Used in the sitting between the two prostrations and in the first Tashahhud (recorded in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 579a**). 83  
* **Tawarruk:** Sitting with the left thigh flat on the ground, pushing the left foot out from under the right leg, and sitting directly on the left buttock. Used in the final Tashahhud sitting of a 3 or 4 Rakah prayer (recorded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 828**). 64, 74, 84  
* **The Tashahhud Finger Gesture:** Pointing the index finger of the right hand toward the Qiblah, raising or moving it slightly during the recitation of the Tashahhud, as recorded in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 579a/579b** and **Sunan an-Nasa'i, Hadith 1219**. 83, 85, 86

### 4\. What You Can Avoid: Common Errors & Prohibited Actions

To ensure the spiritual and physical integrity of the prayer, worshippers must avoid these common errors and detested actions:

* **Audible Intention:** Uttering verbal formulas of intention (e.g., *"I intend to pray..."*) is a religious innovation (*bid'ah*). The intention is strictly an act of the heart 10, 87\.  
* **Fidgeting and Moving Excessively (Amal Kathir):** Continuous non-prayer movements (adjusting clothing, checking watches) without genuine necessity can invalidate the prayer, as it violates the tranquility commanded in **Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 238**: *"And stand before Allah in total devotion."* 23, 88, 89  
* **Incomplete Sujood:** Lifting feet, toes, or hands off the ground during prostration, or failing to let the nose touch the floor firmly alongside the forehead. This violates **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 812**, which mandates prostrating on all seven bones 35, 37\.  
* **Unnecessary Eye Closing:** Closing the eyes is generally disliked (*makrooh*) in the Sunnah unless there is a severe distraction directly in front of the worshipper 67\.  
* **Spreading Arms Like a Dog:** Spreading the forearms flat on the ground during Sujood. Grounded in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 822**: *"Straighten yourselves when doing Sujud, never spread your arms on the ground as dogs do."* 90  
* **Looking Around/Upwards:** Looking up at the sky or looking side-to-side during prayer is strictly forbidden, as warned in **Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 751**: *"It is a way of stealing by which Satan takes away (a portion) from the Salat."* 67  
* **Swaying and Stepping:** Repeated non-prayer movements that disrupt stillness and *khushu'* (humility) 23, 91\.  
* **Failing to Use a Sutrah:** Praying without placing a barrier closely in front of you when praying alone, as commanded in **Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 954**: *"When one of you prays, let him pray toward a sutrah..."* 92, 93  
* **Wiping the Face Post-Taslim:** Immediately wiping the forehead with the hand right after completing the salam has no basis in the authentic Sunnah 94\.  
* **Verbal/Physical Gestures in Salam:** Pointing the hands to the right and left while turning the head for Taslim is strictly prohibited. Grounded in **Sahih Muslim, Hadith 431**: *"What is wrong with them that they make salutation with their hands as if they were the tails of headstrong horses? Place your hand on your thigh..."* 91, 95
